Wait, I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch your last message, like one human talking to another:

- Give me the missing context first: where we are, what you did or found, and why it matters.
- Say it simply and concisely, in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: short sentences, one idea each, no jargon.
- Use the ubiquitous language from `CONTEXT.md` (follow `CONTEXT-MAP.md` to the right one if the repo has more than one). Where it has no term, use plain words.
- If you need something from me, end with that one question.
